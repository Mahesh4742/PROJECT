Vision

To build strong learning and leadership in the field of Computer Science &Engg by strengthening fundamentals and facilitators interdisciplinary sustainable study to meet the ever growing needs of the society and industry.

  • To educate students in technology competencies by providing professionally committed faculty and staff

  • To develop creative environment capable for interdisciplinary study and skill enhancement
  • Develop spirit of innovation and creativity by establishing centres of excellence in upcoming areas.
  • Provide formal and informal technical education to meet dynamic learning.
  • Build up the bridge between industry and students by imparting knowledge and skills to students.
  • Provide ethical and moral standards to face the challenges of the world
